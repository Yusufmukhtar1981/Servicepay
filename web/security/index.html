Account security and recovery

Protect your ServicePay account and use secure password or PIN recovery.

Create accountSign in

Protect your credentials

Never share passwords, transaction PINs, OTPs, full BVN, NIN or card details in search, email or AI chat. ServicePay public help does not request them.

Password recovery

Use the app's existing password-recovery flow and the verified contact channel it offers. Customer identity verification remains mandatory. Authorized Head Office recovery requires explicit permissions and fresh administrator authentication, not a second-person approval.

PIN recovery

Only the customer should set a new transaction PIN through the secure recovery flow after the required stronger verification. Public help cannot reset a PIN, unlock compliance restrictions or approve recovery.

Suspicious activity

Contact support through the official app or support@servicepay.ng. Describe the issue without disclosing secret credentials. Do not follow instructions to transfer money to verify your identity.
