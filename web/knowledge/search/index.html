    Need help with a payment or your account?

    Public guides cannot see your transactions, and nobody here will ask for them. For anything about your money, sign in first.

    1. Sign in to ServicePay.
    2. Open All Services, then AI Support for English or Hausa AI chat and to select your own transaction.
    3. For complaints, open the dashboard Help & support button, then create a ticket.
