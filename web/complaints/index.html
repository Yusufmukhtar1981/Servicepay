Complaints, tickets and human support

Create a secure ServicePay complaint and keep its reference.

Create accountSign in

Create and track a ticket

Sign in, open Help & Support and use the existing complaint form. Choose the appropriate category and explain the issue without secrets. The application returns a ticket reference after successful creation. Track and reply to your own tickets in the app.

AI and human assistance

Open All Services, then AI Support for English or Hausa text assistance where enabled. For an unresolved issue use its human escalation option or the dashboard Help & support button. Account-specific investigation requires authentication and selecting your own record.

Public page feedback

A helpful or not-helpful vote on a public guide is only a local browsing preference, not a complaint or an acknowledgment from the support team. To notify support, create an authenticated ticket or send an email to support@servicepay.ng.

Response expectations

Do not assume a response time, refund or compensation that has not been confirmed through the authorized support process.
