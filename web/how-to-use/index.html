How to use ServicePay

The same simple pattern works across most services.

Create accountSign in

Making a purchase

  1. Sign in to ServicePay (or create an account) at servicepay.ng.
  2. Choose the service, then enter or select the recipient and pick the plan, bundle or amount shown in the app.
  3. Review the actual price and details shown on the confirmation screen before you continue.
  4. Authorize the payment with your PIN or the verification the app asks for.
  5. Check History. History is the authoritative record of whether the transaction succeeded, failed or is pending.

Pending or unclear results

If a transaction shows as pending or the result is unclear, do not blindly retry the same purchase. Wait for History to update, then contact support@servicepay.ng with the reference shown in History if it does not resolve.

Applications and requests

Solar, Empowerment and Amana are applications or requests that are reviewed. Follow status in the app; approval is not guaranteed.

Keep your account safe

  • Never share your password, PIN or verification codes.
  • ServicePay support will not ask for your PIN.
