Delivery with ServicePay

Request delivery of items and follow progress in the app.

Create accountSign in

What Delivery is

ServicePay delivery lets signed-in customers request delivery of items, including marketplace orders where delivery is offered. Fees, if any, are shown in the app before you confirm.

How to use Delivery

  1. Sign in and open Delivery or your marketplace order.
  2. Enter pickup and drop-off details.
  3. Review the fee and details shown.
  4. Confirm and follow status in the app.

Privacy

You only see your own delivery records.

Availability

Availability depends on your account status, service provider, the current catalogue, any required approval and your location. A feature appearing in the app does not mean it is open to every customer at every moment.
