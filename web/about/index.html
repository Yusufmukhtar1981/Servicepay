Who we are

ServicePay is owned and operated by YUMPAY GLOBAL TECH. LTD, based in Kano, Kano State, Nigeria. The platform brings everyday services together in one account with a clear History of each activity.

What you can do

  • Buy mobile data and airtime
  • Pay for electricity
  • Shop on the marketplace and request delivery
  • Request NIN verification
  • Apply for programmes such as Solar, Empowerment and Amana

Availability depends on your account status, service provider, the current catalogue, any required approval and your location. A feature appearing in the app does not mean it is open to every customer at every moment.
