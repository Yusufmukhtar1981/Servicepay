About ServicePay

Useful services, made closer to home.

ServicePay is a Nigerian platform for digital services, payments, commerce, rewards and logistics. We are building a practical place for people to get everyday things done.

Our point of view

Built with Nigerian context in mind.

ServicePay is operated by Yumpay Global Tech Ltd (RC 1895531), with its home in Kano, Nigeria. The platform brings together the services customers choose to use, with clear flows and support when something needs attention.

Our work spans digital payments and wallet activity, everyday services, buying and selling, delivery and logistics.
