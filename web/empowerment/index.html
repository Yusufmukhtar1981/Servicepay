Empowerment and grants

Apply to open empowerment programmes from the app.

Create accountSign in

What Empowerment is

When an empowerment or grant programme is open, eligible customers can apply in the app. Applications are reviewed against each programme's eligibility rules. Approved and paid benefits appear in the app. Applying does not guarantee selection, funding or returns.

How to use Empowerment

  1. Sign in and open Empowerment.
  2. Read the programme requirements.
  3. Submit your application.
  4. Track the review status in the app.

Availability

Availability depends on your account status, service provider, the current catalogue, any required approval and your location. A feature appearing in the app does not mean it is open to every customer at every moment.
