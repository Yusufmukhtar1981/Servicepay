ServicePay Marketplace

Browse products from listed sellers and order from your account.

Create accountSign in

What Marketplace is

The marketplace lists products from sellers on ServicePay. Product availability, prices and delivery options are set per listing and shown in the app.

How to use Marketplace

  1. Sign in and open Marketplace.
  2. Browse and add items.
  3. Review the price and delivery option shown.
  4. Confirm and follow the order status.

Availability

Availability depends on your account status, service provider, the current catalogue, any required approval and your location. A feature appearing in the app does not mean it is open to every customer at every moment.
