Transaction status and troubleshooting

Understand successful, pending and failed transactions without risking duplicate purchases.

Create accountSign in

SUCCESSFUL

Review the transaction details in History. If service delivery is missing despite a successful record, report that record through authenticated support; a public assistant cannot independently confirm delivery.

PENDING or processing

The outcome is not yet definitive. Refresh History and avoid repeating the same uncertain purchase. A pending record must not be treated as proof of success, failure or refund. A separate new purchase still requires its own confirmation and authorization in the app.

FAILED

Read the transaction and wallet records in your own account. A failed label by itself does not prove that a refund is due or has arrived. Support can review the linked records. Never rely on public search to authorize a refund.

Create a complaint

After signing in, open Help & Support, create a ticket and select the relevant record where offered. Keep the complaint reference returned by the application. Do not send PINs, passwords or OTPs.
