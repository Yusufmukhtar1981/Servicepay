Account and Wallet help

Understand account access, wallet records and safe funding troubleshooting.

Create accountSign in

Check your own records

Sign in to your own account to view the Wallet and Transaction History. Public search cannot read your balance or confirm that a deposit arrived.

Funding not reflected

Compare the funding status and reference shown in the app with your bank record. Do not blindly repeat an uncertain transfer. Use Help & Support to create a ticket for the support team. Never post your account number, balance or banking details in public search.

Secure support

AI Smart Support can guide you and, where enabled, investigate a record you select after authentication. A stored status is not necessarily fresh provider confirmation. AI cannot move money or promise a refund.
