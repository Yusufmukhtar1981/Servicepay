What happens

1. Ownership verification

Every request must be verified with an email code or your current password. Requests that cannot be verified are not processed.

2. Review

Every verified request is reviewed manually by ServicePay Head Office. Unresolved wallet balances, orders, deliveries, financing, disputes or legal holds require review before deletion can complete. Support may contact you through your registered details.

3. Deletion

On approval, ServicePay automatically erases eligible profile and sign-in details and saved contacts, and signs you out of all sessions. Financial records are preserved.

4. Connected systems and partners

Before marking the request complete, Head Office reviews other connected ServicePay systems and documents what was erased there, any deletion requests sent to payment processors or other partners, the records kept for legal reasons, the legal policy that applies and when retained records will be reviewed again. Partner deletion is not automatic; partners act under their own obligations.

Timing

Processing deadline: 30 calendar days after your ownership is verified. If an unresolved obligation or legal hold affects your request, ServicePay Support will explain it to you.

What we must keep

Nigerian law and financial regulation require some records to be retained after account deletion. These are kept only as long as required and are not used for marketing.

  • Transaction, wallet, payment and withdrawal records
  • KYC and identity verification records
  • Fraud-prevention, security and audit logs
  • Records needed for disputes, legal claims or regulator requests

Required records remain in financial and audit systems under existing access controls; they are not part of your active customer profile. Retention periods depend on the record type and applicable law. If an unresolved obligation or legal hold applies, Support will explain it.

Payment processors, banks and other partners hold their own records under their own legal obligations and privacy notices.
