Solar applications

Submit a solar application from the app for review.

Create accountSign in

What Solar is

Customers can submit a solar application in the app. Applications are reviewed, and some approved applications may be offered a solar finance arrangement. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval, financing, pricing or installation dates. Terms of any approved arrangement are provided to you at that stage.

How to use Solar

  1. Sign in and open Solar.
  2. Complete the application form.
  3. Submit and wait for review.
  4. Track your application status in the app.

Availability

Availability depends on your account status, service provider, the current catalogue, any required approval and your location. A feature appearing in the app does not mean it is open to every customer at every moment.
