Availability

Availability depends on your account status, service provider, the current catalogue, any required approval and your location. A feature appearing in the app does not mean it is open to every customer at every moment.

Other features and availability

The current customer interface does not promote transfers, withdrawals, cable TV, exam PINs or airtime-to-cash as available services. This does not remove historical records. Flight information, where shown, does not include actual ticket booking. Other enabled services, including Keke Napep transport, may appear in All Services; check their current availability after signing in.
