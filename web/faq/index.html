Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about using ServicePay.

Create accountSign in
What is ServicePay?

A Nigerian digital services app owned and operated by YUMPAY GLOBAL TECH. LTD, based in Kano.

How do I create an account?

Go to servicepay.ng/?mode=register and follow the steps. See the registration guide.

Is every service available to everyone?

Availability depends on your account status, service provider, the current catalogue, any required approval and your location. A feature appearing in the app does not mean it is open to every customer at every moment.

My purchase is pending. Should I try again?

If a transaction shows as pending or the result is unclear, do not blindly retry the same purchase. Wait for History to update, then contact support@servicepay.ng with the reference shown in History if it does not resolve.

Will I get a virtual bank account instantly?

Not necessarily. Creation of new virtual accounts with our current provider is temporarily unavailable, so we do not promise instant account numbers.

Can I send money or withdraw to a bank?

Wallet transfers and withdrawals are not currently offered to customers in the app.

Can I book flights?

No. ServicePay does not currently book flight tickets.

Is there an iPhone app?

No iOS app is currently announced. Use the web app at servicepay.ng or the Android app.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps on the delete account page.

How do I contact support?

Email support@servicepay.ng or call +234 913 615 1515.
