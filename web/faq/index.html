Questions, answered

Good to know before you begin.

A short guide to ServicePay, official support and account safety.

What is ServicePay?

ServicePay is the official Nigerian digital services, fintech, commerce, rewards and logistics platform operated by Yumpay Global Tech Ltd (RC 1895531).

Where is ServicePay available in Nigeria?

ServicePay is a Nigerian platform operated from Kano. Individual services may have their own availability and location requirements.

Who operates ServicePay?

ServicePay is operated by Yumpay Global Tech Ltd (RC 1895531).

Where can I use ServicePay?

You can use the ServicePay experiences available for your account and location, as shown in the app.

How do I access ServicePay?

Use the official ServicePay app or website and follow the account access flow provided there.

How do promotions and rewards work?

Promotion and rewards mechanics, qualification requirements and applicable terms are shown with the relevant ServicePay experience. Qualification does not guarantee winning; terms apply.

How do I contact official support?

Email admin@servicepay.ng or call +234 913 615 1515. The public address support@servicepay.ng is also available for customer questions.

What services are available?

Available experiences include wallet and payment, bills and digital services, marketplace, delivery, logistics, solar, financing, transport, Mini Apps and rewards.

Will ServicePay ask for my PIN or OTP?

Do not send your password, transaction PIN or one-time code through ordinary support messages.

How do I request account deletion?

Use the account deletion and data request page.

Where can I read about data use?

Read the ServicePay Privacy Policy for information about collection, use, sharing, retention and choices.
